## Soul Shards

How do I create infinite copies of me.  I can't. Today though, I can create near infinite manifestations of my intentions. A command in a command line interface - `Do the Thing!` - and the machine goes burrrrr.

It's getting a lot better at doing the thing, but it still doesn't do it like me. Not that I do it perfectly either, mind you, but at least I own "the thing". I made it, it is a product of me.

If I could capture the _essence_, that key part of myself, responsible for giving the thing it's unique _me-ness_ into an instruction, then the machine could create infinite outputs that are - again, in _essence_ - a product of me.

```
Do the Thing like I would
```

This feels important. It also feels dangerous. How do I capture this essence and share it with a genAI model such as they exist today - language based stochastic parrots? I'd have to do it in writing and hope nothing gets lost in translation. Yet writing feels like such an inadequate tool to capture my essence. It's one version of the map, but certainly not the territory. Maybe if I define the part that I'm trying to capture in a small enough way, maybe that might work.

Maybe I can create a `Soul Shard`. A little document that represents a small part of my essence, one for any given domain. I could end up with many little `Soul Shards`: how would I approach problem A; how would I realise plan B. Then I can release infinite copies of me to act on that narrow domain!

I mentioned it felt dangerous. What if I miscaptured my essence. I'm still responsible for the output, It's still mine. Now I'm outputting work I cannot stand behind and proudly claim as my own, and I'm putting it out into the world.

You see, unlike in code, where I can declare:
> This function takes in two parameters and multiplies them
> check that if i put in 2 and 2, it outputs 4
> check that if i put in 3 and 0, it outputs 0
> check that ...
> etc...

I can't do that with my `Soul Shard`. "Check that this Soul Shard accurately represents my essence" isn't a verifiable statement.

## Selling my soul to a comercial enterprise.

Let's say I do create these little `Soul Shards`, and I use the current crop of GenAI tools to execute my intentions inline with the essence described within. Up to this point, I've only had to deal with the issue of losing parts of myself in translating my essence to language. Now we also have to deal with the fact that these algorithms are owned my commercial entities, with financial incentives. The wind always seem to blow in the direction of selling my attention. If Company X can truly know who I am, then they can sell me the product of my dreams. More nefariously, if they truly know who I am, they can mould my dreams to the shape of a product placed by yet another commercial entity.

This smells odd, fishy. Selling a part of my soul in exchange for infinite output. How's that for an exchange.
